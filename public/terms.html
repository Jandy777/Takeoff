Terms of use

Updated November 11, 2026

These usage guidelines describe Takeoff, West Suburban Concrete’s foundation drawing and estimating application.

Accounts and access

Use your own account and accurate account information. New accounts begin with viewing access. The administrator controls editing, administrator permissions, and whether an account can access the application.

Shared project information

Use the library for project information you are authorized to provide and share. Registered users can view the shared drawings and project details. Keep access credentials private and contact the administrator if access needs to change.

Drawings and quantities

Takeoff helps users recreate foundation layouts and calculate material quantities from the information they enter. Review dimensions, wall details, revisions, and quantities against the current project plans before using them for quotes, ordering, delivery, or field work. A Takeoff drawing does not replace the project’s approved construction documents.

Project history and data

Keep revision labels clear so other users can identify the correct drawing. The privacy policy explains account information, shared drawing data, and browser recovery storage.

Questions

For account access or application questions, contact jake@concretews.com.
