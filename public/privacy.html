TAKEOFF West Suburban Concrete

Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026

Takeoff is West Suburban Concrete’s foundation drawing and estimating application. This page describes the information used by the application.

Account information

Takeoff uses your email address and account identifier to sign you in and manage access. Employee signup also records the employee name you select. Your account email, employee request, and access level are available to the application administrator.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic identity information, including your account identifier, email address, and profile information. Supabase handles this sign-in information. Takeoff requests only basic sign-in scopes and uses this information for authentication and account access.

Drawings and project details

The cloud library stores drawing geometry, appearance settings, and project details entered by users, including customer, project, city, estimator, model, elevation, and revision information. Source blueprint PDFs are not uploaded or stored by this application.

The library is shared: registered accounts can view drawings and project details. Administrators control editing and account access. Drawings and revisions are kept for future project reference.

Services and device storage

Takeoff uses Supabase for authentication and cloud drawing data, and Netlify for website hosting. These services receive information needed to deliver and operate the application, including connection details. Their handling of information is described in the Supabase privacy policy and Netlify privacy statement.

Your browser stores sign-in session information and drawing recovery drafts. Exporting or printing a drawing creates a separate copy that is handled outside the cloud library.

Account changes and questions

Contact jake@concretews.com to request account changes or deletion, ask about stored project records, or raise a privacy question. Project records may need to remain available for company project history.
